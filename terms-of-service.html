1. Acceptance of Terms & Eligibility

By registering an account, listing a property, submitting a tenant application, or accessing the PropVantage dashboard, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agree to be bound by these Terms of Service.

You must be at least 18 years of age and possess the legal authority to enter into binding contracts. If you are registering on behalf of a corporation, LLC, or trust, you warrant that you hold express authorization to bind that entity.

2. User Accounts & Responsibilities

  • Property Owners (Landlords): Warrant that they possess lawful title or authorized management rights to listed properties, and that premises comply with all local habitability codes.
  • Tenants & Applicants: Agree to provide accurate financial and identity information during application screening and adhere strictly to digitally signed lease provisions.
  • Account Credentials: You are strictly responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of your credentials and for all activities occurring under your account.

3. Management Fees, Rent Collection & Payouts

PropVantage charges management fees according to your selected plan (Basic, Professional, or Enterprise) as published on our Pricing page or in your customized Master Property Agreement.

  • Automated Rent Collection: Rents are collected from tenants on the 1st of each month via ACH direct debit or credit card.
  • Owner Disbursements: Rental disbursements to landlord accounts are initiated on the 8th–10th business day following payment settlement, net of management fees and approved maintenance costs.
  • Late Fees: Standard statutory late fees are automatically assessed on delinquent tenant payments after grace periods defined in local ordinances.

Security Deposit Escrow: All security deposits collected are segregated into state-compliant, interest-bearing escrow trust accounts and returned in accordance with statutory deadlines.

4. Maintenance Coordination & Repairs

PropVantage maintains a 24/7 bilingual maintenance coordination desk to protect owner assets and ensure rapid resolution of tenant issues.

  • Pre-Authorized Repair Limit: Routine repairs up to the standard limit ($350 or custom configured amount) are automatically dispatched to licensed, insured contractors to prevent property degradation.
  • Major Work Orders: Any repair exceeding the pre-authorized limit requires explicit landlord approval via the digital Owner Portal, except in bona fide emergencies (e.g. gas leaks, flooding).

5. Fair Housing & Regulatory Compliance

PropVantage rigorously adheres to the Federal Fair Housing Act, Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA), and state and municipal human rights laws. We do not discriminate on the basis of race, color, religion, sex, national origin, familial status, disability, sexual orientation, or source of income.

6. Limitation of Liability & Indemnification

To the maximum extent permitted by applicable law, PropVantage shall not be liable for indirect, incidental, special, or consequential damages resulting from tenant lease defaults, contractor delays, or temporary portal service interruptions.

Property owners agree to maintain adequate property liability and hazard insurance coverage on all managed premises naming PropVantage as additional insured where standard.

7. Cancellation & Termination

Landlords on standard month-to-month plans may terminate property management services with 30 days written notice via the Owner Portal. In the event of termination:

  • All active lease agreements, tenant files, and key transfers will be promptly finalized.
  • Final accounting statements and remaining escrow balances will be reconciled within 15 business days.
