1. Information We Collect

PropVantage Inc. ("PropVantage", "we", "us", or "our") collects information necessary to provide comprehensive real estate management, tenant placement, automated rent distribution, and owner portal services.

A. Information Provided Directly by You:
  • Property Owner Data: Full legal name, government-issued ID, tax identification (SSN/EIN), property deeds, bank routing information for rental payouts, and insurance policies.
  • Tenant & Applicant Data: Contact details, employment history, pay stubs, credit score reports, background check authorizations, and emergency contact details.
  • Communication Records: Support tickets, maintenance dispatches, SMS notifications, and portal messages between landlords, tenants, and contractors.
B. Automatically Collected Technical Data:
  • Device information, IP address, browser type, operating system, portal access timestamps, and session logs.

Note on Sensitive Financial Data: All banking and payment card details are tokenized and processed via PCI-DSS Level 1 certified processors. PropVantage never stores raw credit card numbers on local servers.

2. How We Use Your Information

We process your data strictly under lawful bases including contract fulfillment, legitimate interest, and compliance with statutory housing and tax laws:

  • Executing digital lease agreements and enforcing tenant policy rules.
  • Facilitating monthly rent payments, security deposit escrow handling, and owner cashflow payouts.
  • Conducting fair housing-compliant background verification and tenant screening.
  • Coordinating emergency maintenance, licensed contractor dispatches, and property inspections.
  • Generating tax-ready annual 1099 packages and itemized profit-and-loss reports for property owners.
  • Preventing fraud, unauthorized logins, and lease agreement defaults.

3. Information Sharing & Third-Party Disclosures

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes. Disclosures are limited to trusted partners strictly necessary for operations:

  • Screening & Credit Bureaus: TransUnion, Experian, and Equifax for applicant background and credit checks upon tenant authorization.
  • Payment Gateways & Banks: NACHA-compliant ACH networks and banking partners for direct deposits.
  • Maintenance Contractors: Vetted third-party repair vendors who require tenant contact details solely to arrange property access for scheduled repairs.
  • Legal & Regulatory Authorities: When required by court order, eviction proceedings, or statutory audit requirements.

4. Data Storage, Retention & Security

Your data is stored in tier-4 data centers with 256-bit AES encryption at rest and TLS 1.3 encryption in transit. We maintain redundant backups across geographically isolated secure cloud environments.

We retain your rental records for as long as your account is active, and for a statutory period of 7 years thereafter to comply with state real estate licensing, IRS tax documentation, and tenancy record-keeping statutes.

5. Your Legal Rights (GDPR & CCPA/CPRA)

Depending on your jurisdiction, you possess specific statutory rights regarding your personal information:

  • Right to Access: Request a complete export of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to Rectification: Request correction of inaccurate property or contact records.
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): Request deletion of non-essential records (subject to legal lease & tax retention obligations).
  • Right to Opt-Out: Unsubscribe from non-essential promotional communications at any time.
  • Non-Discrimination: We will never deny services or adjust pricing if you exercise your privacy rights.

6. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

We use essential cookies to maintain user authentication sessions, preserve dark/light theme preferences, and protect against Cross-Site Request Forgery (CSRF). We do not utilize invasive cross-site advertising trackers.
