1. Cloud Infrastructure & Data Encryption

PropVantage leverages Tier-4 cloud infrastructure distributed across high-availability data centers with 99.99% uptime SLAs.

  • Encryption at Rest: All databases, documents, lease agreements, and file storage use AES-256 encryption managed via hardware security modules (HSM).
  • Encryption in Transit: All HTTP traffic is enforced over TLS 1.3 with HSTS (HTTP Strict Transport Security) and automated certificate rotation.
  • Continuous Disaster Recovery: Automated point-in-time recovery snapshots replicated across geographically isolated availability zones.

2. Financial Escrow & Banking Security

We implement rigid accounting controls to protect owner payouts, tenant rent transactions, and security deposits:

  • PCI-DSS Level 1 Compliance: Credit card and debit transactions are processed with strict PCI compliance. Payment tokens never expose sensitive card numbers to backend databases.
  • NACHA Certified ACH Processing: Direct bank account transfers are validated with instant micro-deposit and open-banking API protocols.
  • Dual-Authorization Payouts: Large disbursements and vendor invoices undergo automated multi-signature verification prior to fund release.

FDIC Insured Trust Accounts: Every dollar in tenant security deposits is held in dedicated trust accounts with participating member FDIC financial institutions.

3. Identity Verification & Multi-Factor Access

To eliminate unauthorized access, our platform utilizes modern zero-trust architecture:

  • Mandatory Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA): Available via Authenticator apps (TOTP), SMS OTP, and hardware security keys (WebAuthn/FIDO2).
  • Role-Based Access Control (RBAC): Granular permissions ensuring property managers, maintenance coordinators, and financial auditors access only minimum requisite data.
  • Session Invalidation: Automatic timeout and instant revocation of compromised active sessions across all devices.

4. Regulatory Housing & Privacy Compliance

Our operational policies undergo continuous legal review to ensure total compliance across federal and regional statutory bodies:

  • Federal Fair Housing Act (FHA): Standardized tenant screening criteria that prevents systemic bias or unlawful housing discrimination.
  • Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA): Accessible portal interfaces adhering to WCAG 2.1 AA digital accessibility standards.
  • GDPR & CCPA/CPRA: Full data subject rights implementation including comprehensive data export, privacy controls, and automated retention lifecycle management.

5. 24/7 Threat Monitoring & Incident Response

Our dedicated Security Operations Center (SOC) continuously monitors system events using advanced intrusion detection and behavioral telemetry:

  • DDoS Mitigation: Multi-layer DDoS mitigation capable of filtering volumetric attacks in real-time.
  • Web Application Firewall (WAF): Automated blocking of OWASP Top 10 exploits (SQL Injection, XSS, SSRF).
  • Incident SLA: Critical severity vulnerabilities or potential compromises trigger immediate containment workflows within 15 minutes of detection.
